## I. Executive Architecture

**Fundamental constraints:**
- All players are **Ironman**. Trading disabled.
- Start in **Misthalin + Havenhythe** (2 regions, 191 tasks, 15,490 points).
- **Karamja** auto-unlocks at **50 tasks**.
- **3 additional regions** unlock at **175 / 300 / 450 tasks** (your choice each threshold).
- **6 regions total** → determines which of the 1,152 tasks you can complete.
- **7 relic tiers** → skilling/combat acceleration path.
- **8 blessing tiers** (3 paths: Order / Balance / Chaos) → combat identity and God Tier rewards.
- **Dragon Trophy** at **48,000 points**.
- **Damage cap (30k) removed**. **LP cap removed**.

## II. Relic Tier Analysis

### Tier 1 (10 pts) — The Foundation Choice

| Relic | Archetype | Best For |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Endless Harvest** | Passive gatherer | AFK play, Archaeology focus, multi-skill alt |
| **Survivalist** | Active gatherer | Efficiency mains, Mining/WC optimization, rockertunity abuse |
| **Golden Touch** | Money/Agility | Thieving rush, Prayer funding, early-game wealth |

**Sovereign recommendation:** If your region path includes **Desert** or **Kandarin** (high Thieving content), **Golden Touch** funds everything. If you plan to rush Archaeology for relic powers, **Survivalist** edges out Endless Harvest due to the 50% auto-restore chance + time sprite doubling. **Endless Harvest** is the AFK default.

**Clarifications (post-reveal):**
- Survivalist: **XP is NOT awarded for extra yields**. Tools are named: Pickaxe of the Faithful, Harpoon of the Pious, Hatchet of Devotion.
- Golden Touch: Feathers **cannot** be offered to altars, bonfires, or Ectofuntus.

### Tier 2 (750 pts) — Production vs. Gathering

| Relic | Archetype | Best For |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Animal Wrangler** | Farming/Hunter | POF/Anachronia Dino Farm rush, Big Game Hunter |
| **Superheated** | Smithing/Firemaking | Masterwork prep, burial armor XP, invention materials |
| **Divine Druid** | Herblore/Summoning | Potion factory, charmless summoning, divination alt |

**Sovereign recommendation:** **Superheated** is the highest-impact tier 2 for melee mains. It generates invention materials passively via smithing, enables zero-burn cooking, and the burial set XP ×3 stacks with the 8× league multiplier for absurd Smithing gains. If you pick **Anachronia**, **Animal Wrangler** becomes mandatory for the Dino Farm. **Divine Druid** is the Herblore/Summoning power play — Thera's Pouch replaces spirit shards entirely.

### Tier 3 (1,750 pts) — Mobility & Slayer

| Relic | Archetype | Best For |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Nature's Network** | Farming teleport | Herb runs, tree runs, instant crop completion |
| **Assassin's Insight** | Slayer acceleration | Task choice, elite spawn abuse, ushabti farming |
| **Voidwalker** | Jewellery omnitool | Clue scrolls, skilling loot boxes, universal teleports |

**Sovereign recommendation:** **Voidwalker** is the "correct" choice for any build planning to do clues or needing teleport coverage across limited regions. The abyssal conduit replaces 13+ teleport items. **Assassin's Insight** only wins if you're hard-rushing Slayer logs for completionism. **Nature's Network** is for pure skilling mains who want zero-friction farming.

---

### Tier 4 (3,500 pts) — The Power Spike

| Relic | Archetype | Best For |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Crystal Grace** | Magic/Necromancy | All spellbooks unlocked, ritual automation, RC ×3 output |
| **Transmutation** | Resource alchemy | Converting region-locked resources, bypassing unlocks |
| **Antiquarian** | Archaeology max | Monolith 1,000 energy, 10× chronotes, ancient caskets |

**Sovereign recommendation:** **Crystal Grace** is the combat power spike. Unlocking all spellbooks + auto-glyphs for Necromancy rituals removes the biggest friction in the skill. The 5% crystal essence chance replaces pure/impure essence entirely. **Transmutation** is niche but critical if you need resources from regions you didn't unlock (e.g., converting lower-tier ores to higher). **Antiquarian** is only for Archaeology completionists — but the 10× Chronotes (replacing T1's 5×) + ancient caskets is massive for cash and materials.

**Clarifications:**
- Antiquarian 10× Chronotes **replaces** (does not stack with) tier 1's 5× bonus.
- Lore page bonuses **do stack**: 30% (T2 passive) + 20% (Antiquarian) = **50% total**.

### Tier 5 (6,000 pts) — Quality of Life vs. Production

| Relic | Archetype | Best For |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Clue Connoisseur** | Clue completion | Globetrotter, min steps, max rolls, no clue limit |
| **Production Master** | All production | Brooch of Spirits, +6 boosts, process-all, no burns |
| **Devout** | Summoning max | Bank anywhere, scrollless familiars, 500% familiar damage |

**Sovereign recommendation:** **Production Master** is the default. The +6 invisible boost to 6 production skills stacks with spicy stews and other boosts for massive XP rates. Brooch of Spirits replaces portable skilling stations. **Devout** is only for Summoning-focused builds (e.g., if you need a specific familiar for a boss). **Clue Connoisseur** is for completionists — but with NO LIMIT on sealed clues and Globetrotter charges 3× faster, it's actually the fastest way to generate wealth from TT Points.

---

### Tier 6 (12,000 pts) — The Flex Tier

| Relic | Archetype | Best For |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Perkfection** | Invention max | 0% junk, 10× materials, no charge drain, machine L1 |
| **Rejuvenated** | Second relic | Taking a missed tier 1–5 relic |

**Sovereign recommendation:** **Perkfection** if you use augmented gear (which you should). The 0% junk chance + 10× materials means you can disassemble cheap items for components at ludicrous rates. No charge drain on augmented items removes the biggest gold sink in the game. Machines buildable from level 1 and running 10× faster is absurd for passive skilling. **Rejuvenated** is for correcting a mistake — e.g., taking Endless Harvest at T1 then realizing you need Survivalist, or doubling down on Production Master for the +6 boost + Brooch.

**Rejuvenated mechanics:**
- You **keep** Perkfection's passives (XP 16×, rare 8×, all Seren spells/prayers).
- You **also** get one previous tier relic (1–5) fully active.
- This is a **bypass** of the normal 1-pick-per-tier rule.

---

### Tier 7 (20,000 pts) — The Capstone

| Relic | Archetype | Best For |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Infernal Fire** | DPS execution | Death Mark 100% proc, adrenaline on kill, +18.7 style bonus |
| **Naragi Edict** | Survivability | Heal 40k over 16.8s, 255 combat levels, revive on death |
| **Icyenic Faith** | Prayer tank | +50 Prayer bonus, crit scaling, 100% protection prayer block |

**Sovereign recommendation:** For your melee Zuk build (Scourge + Drygore, Biting 4, Aftershock 4, Devoted 4, etc.), **Infernal Fire** is the DPS capstone. Death Mark execution at 20% HP with 5% adrenaline refund creates a snowball loop. **Icyenic Faith** is the tank option — with +50 Prayer bonus, you're looking at +10% crit chance and +10% base ability damage from the tome alone, plus 100% protection prayer block that also Soul Splits. This is actually higher sustained DPS than Infernal Fire in long encounters if you can maintain prayer.

**Naragi Edict** is the "I don't want to die" button. The revive + 255 combat levels + 40k healing is unmatched for learning new bosses or hardcore pushing. The pocket slot alone (+300 armour, +14 damage, +1,500 LP) is worth the pick for any tank build.

## III. Blessing Path Analysis

Blessings are combat-only and unlock via Blessing Tasks (combat achievements, boss kills, Slayer tasks). Each region has 5 Blessing Tasks. With 6 regions, you have 30 tasks available and need **26 for God Tier 2**.

**Blessing resets granted at:** Tier 1, God Tier 1, God Tier 2. Use these to pivot your path if your build evolves.

---

### Path: Chaos (Zamorak)

**Identity:** High adrenaline, high crit, glass cannon execution.

**Key synergies:**
```
Adrenaline Junkie (T1) → Avernic Rampage (T3) → Unholy Critual (T5) → Perfidious (T6)
```

With **Infernal Fire** relic: Death Mark executes at 20% → Avernic Rampage refunds adrenaline → you never stop ulting.

**Chaotic Insight (God Tier 2):** Each gear piece counts as +2 towards set effects. This is insane for partial set bonuses (e.g., 3-piece Cryptbloom effect at 1 piece + 2 phantom pieces).

**Best for:** Melee/ranged burst builds, speedrunners, players comfortable with low defense.

---

### Path: Balance (Guthix)

**Identity:** Sustain, poison, hybrid scaling, True Equilibrium.

**Key synergies:**
```
Big Boned (T1) → Eternal Sustenance (T3) → True Equilibrium (T4) → Tearing Thorns (T5) → Envenomed (T6)
```

**True Equilibrium** rewards diversity: if you pick 1 of each path across tiers, you get the buff once. 2 unique paths = buff twice. 3 unique paths = buff 3×.

| Stacks | Ability Dmg | Armour | LP | Crit % | Crit Dmg % | Prayer |
|--------|-------------|--------|----|--------|------------|--------|
| 1× | +75 | +50 | +500 | +5% | +7.5% | +5 |
| 2× | +150 | +100 | +1,000 | +10% | +15% | +10 |
| 3× | +225 | +150 | +1,500 | +15% | +22.5% | +15 |

**Power Archive (God Tier 2):** Store 20 gizmos in a bot, granting their effects while wearing augmented gear. Combat perks in stored gizmos have rank **doubled**. This means Biting 4 becomes effectively Biting 8, Aftershock 4 becomes Aftershock 8, etc.

**Best for:** Hybrid builds, players who want flexibility, poison mains.

---

### Path: Order (Saradomin)

**Identity:** Tank, cooldown reduction, defensive scaling.

**Key synergies:**
```
Teragard's Aegis (T1) → Striking Light (T2) → Steadfast Will (T3) → Higher Power (T4) → Lord of Light (T5) → Tempered Heart (T6)
```

**Genesis Essence (God Tier 2):** Equipped weapons treated as **tier 120**. Combined with Higher Power's +30% base ability damage, your weapons mathematically hit like **tier 156**.

**Best for:** Tank mains, defensive ability enthusiasts, players who want consistent DPS without burst windows.

## VII. Build Templates

### Template A: Chaos Executioner (Melee Burst)
**Relics:** Survivalist → Superheated → Voidwalker → Crystal Grace → Production Master → Perkfection → Infernal Fire
**Blessings:** Adrenaline Junkie → Abyssal Cinders → Avernic Rampage → Havoc Born → Unholy Critual → Perfidious → Chaotic Insight
**Regions:** Misthalin → Havenhythe → Karamja → Desert → Asgarnia → Wilderness
**Identity:** Death Mark execute snowball. Avernic Rampage refunds adrenaline on every execute. Perfidious makes Inferno of Zamorak proc constantly. Chaotic Insight gives Cryptbloom/Elite Tectonic set effects at low piece counts.

### Template B: Order Immortal (Tank)
**Relics:** Endless Harvest → Divine Druid → Nature's Network → Crystal Grace → Devout → Rejuvenated (Production Master) → Icyenic Faith
**Blessings:** Teragard's Aegis → Striking Light → Steadfast Will → Higher Power → Lord of Light → Tempered Heart → Genesis Essence
**Regions:** Misthalin → Havenhythe → Karamja → Fremennik → Morytania → Tirannwn
**Identity:** 100% protection prayer block + Soul Split + 6% adrenaline/1.2s = you never die. Genesis Essence T120 weapons + Higher Power = tier 156 damage from a tank. Rejuvenated back for Production Master gives +6 boosts and Brooch of Spirits.

### Template C: Balance Poison (DoT Hybrid)
**Relics:** Golden Touch → Animal Wrangler → Assassin's Insight → Antiquarian → Clue Connoisseur → Perkfection → Naragi Edict
**Blessings:** Big Boned → Barkscales → Eternal Sustenance → True Equilibrium → Tearing Thorns → Envenomed → Power Archive
**Regions:** Misthalin → Havenhythe → Karamja → Anachronia → Kandarin → Wilderness
**Identity:** Every 5th DoT triggers Grasp of Guthix. Envenomed +50% poison and disables immunity. True Equilibrium at 3 stacks gives +225 ability damage, +150 armour, +1,500 LP. Power Archive stores 20 gizmos with doubled ranks — your poison perks become monstrous.
